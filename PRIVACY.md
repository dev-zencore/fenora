# Privacy

Finora is designed as an offline-first Android application.

- No account is required.
- No analytics SDK is included.
- No advertising SDK is included.
- No Google Analytics or tracking endpoint is used by the application.
- Financial data is stored locally on the device.
- Network access is not required for the core experience.
- The Obsidian vault is selected and controlled by the user.

Any future integration must preserve these principles and be reviewed in the open source repository before inclusion.
